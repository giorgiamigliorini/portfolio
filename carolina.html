Carolina

Book layout & design
Design and layout for a new biography of nineteenth-century actress Carolina Internari.
Book layout & design Editorial coordination Historical theatre research Publisher: La Conchiglia di Santiago

Carolina Internari (1793–1859) was one of the leading tragic actresses of the Italian stage in the first half of the nineteenth century — a performer from Livorno whose career carried her from the touring companies of a still-divided Italy to a celebrated, turbulent season in Paris.

I worked on the design and layout of La diva che sfidò Parigi. Carolina Internari: Alfieri, Goldoni e l'avventura francese, Giuseppe Donateo's new biography of the actress, published by La Conchiglia di Santiago. Drawing on Internari's own scrapbook of memorabilia — held today at the Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale in Florence — the book follows her through the practical hardships of early-nineteenth-century Italian theatre: companies moving from city to city by coach and boat before the railway, rehearsing on the road, and working through the political upheavals of the Risorgimento. Its central chapters are devoted to the 1830 season in Paris, when Internari's company became the first Italian troupe to perform prose theatre in the French capital since the Revolution.

The book was presented on 26 March at the Archivio di Stato in Livorno, with an introduction by theatre historian Michele Montanelli and a conversation between the author and director and writer Giuseppe Ranucci, alongside publisher Andrea Mancini.
