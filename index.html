About Me

Giorgia Migliorini is an editor, proofreader, copywriter, and layout designer working across print and digital publishing. Her work spans curatorship, from exhibition concept to design and installation, alongside proofreading, translation, and editorial production for publishing houses, cultural foundations and magazines. She writes for her own projects, but also knows how to manage relations with writers and curators. Her editorial and design skills extend into digital publishing and content strategy, from web development and multimedia content to SEO and digital campaigns.

Curator for
“Through the Gaze” — Solo Exhibition of Graziana Ulivieri
Curated by Giorgia Migliorini.
March 29–April 12, 2025.

Organised in collaboration with Ibiskos Ulivieri Publishing House and the Figurative Arts Center of Empoli, exploring one of the most recurring themes in Graziana Ulivieri's artistic production: the representation of women and femininity, and the evolution of her visual language across different phases of her career.
