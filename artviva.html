Artviva

Content strategy & copywriting
From an e-commerce template to a storytelling-driven site, shaped together with the brand.
Client consultation & discovery From e-commerce to storytelling Brand-aligned tone of voice Full content editing & SEO update

Working with Artviva began with listening. Before touching a single line of copy, I sat down with the company's owners to understand their needs and their vision for the brand, and to work out where the existing site was falling short — keeping that conversation open throughout the project, rather than treating the initial brief as fixed.

The starting point was a site built like an online shop: product-first and transactional, with little room for the story behind the brand. Together, we shaped a different direction — a site built around storytelling, restructured to reflect who Artviva is and how they work, and aligned in tone and style with their existing brand identity rather than with a generic e-commerce template.

Alongside the redesign, I took on the editing of all the site's content — copy, descriptions, page structure — updating it for SEO so that the new version could perform at least as well as the one it replaced, without losing the voice we had just built together.
