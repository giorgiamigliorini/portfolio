Digitalization

Fondazione Giuliano Scabia — Archive
Digitising the first 200 folders of Giuliano Scabia's archive, alongside a wider role at the foundation that keeps his work and legacy in public view.
Archive digitisation & cataloguing Digital platform maintenance Grant applications & project development Event organisation & artist liaison

Since 2024, I've been a creative collaborator with the Fondazione Giuliano Scabia in Florence, the foundation set up to preserve and promote the work of the playwright, poet and visual artist Giuliano Scabia. One strand of that collaboration has been the digitisation of the foundation's archive.

I worked through the first 200 folders of the Scabia archive, digitising the material they contain and cataloguing it so it can be searched and consulted properly, then uploading and organising it on the foundation's digital platform, which I also help maintain. It's slow, detail-heavy work, but it's what makes decades of manuscripts, notes and documents usable again — for research now, and for whatever exhibitions or publications come next.

Alongside the archive, my role at the foundation has ranged more widely: collaborating on applications for regional, public and private grants, from the initial idea through to funding and implementation; helping organise the foundation's scheduled events — exhibitions, performances, conferences, presentations; managing relationships with the artists, academics, authors, museums and hosting institutions involved, as well as with event participants and the public; designing and laying out the books, catalogues, panels and flyers the foundation publishes; and promoting its events on Instagram, Facebook and the foundation's own website.
